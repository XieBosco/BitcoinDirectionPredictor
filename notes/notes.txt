Probability of Uniform Direction (5m close)
There is a 60.83% probability that BTC, ETH, SOL, and XRP all close in the same direction (all Green or all Red) at the end of a 5-minute candle.

Probability Altcoins Follow BTC
When BTC has a clear direction, the probability that altcoins close in the same direction is:

ETH/USDT: 82.38%
SOL/USDT: 79.81%
XRP/USDT: 77.67%
BTC Reversal Probability (First 3 mins logic)
When BTC moves in the opposite direction to all three altcoins during the first 3 minutes of a 5-minute candle, there is a 64.67% probability that BTC will reverse and close in the same direction as the altcoins by the end of the 5-minute candle.
(Sample size: 1353 events)

Altcoin Reversal Probability (New Statistic)
When a specific altcoin moves in the opposite direction to BTC during the first 3 minutes of a 5-minute candle, the probability that the altcoin reverses to match BTC's direction by the end is:

ETH: 63.26% (matches BTC)
SOL: 56.95% (matches BTC)
XRP: 54.91% (matches BTC)
Interpretation: ETH is significantly more likely to correct itself to follow BTC than SOL or XRP.

Trendline Reversion
When a pair diverges significantly (> 2 standard deviations) from the group average, the probability of it reverting (moving back toward the average) in the short term is slightly better than a coin flip (~52-54%):

BTC: ~52.2% (5m)
ETH: ~52.0% (5m)
SOL: ~53.6% (5m)
XRP: ~52.0% (5m)
